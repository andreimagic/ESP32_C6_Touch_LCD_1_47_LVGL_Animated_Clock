🎱 Bingo Cards

Printable UK/housie-style bingo tickets — the same generator built into the clock's Web Configuration page, playable here without any hardware.

← Back to Home

How it works

Each ticket is 3 rows × 9 columns with exactly 15 numbers, 5 per row, and every column restricted to its own decade (column 1 = 1–9 … column 9 = 80–90) — the standard layout used to pair with the clock's on-device Bingo! caller (a 1–90 number-caller app in the Apps Menu). Tickets regenerate entirely in your browser, so every load — and every tap of New cards — deals a fresh set. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Tickets per page:

Tickets stay light/paper-styled regardless of the site theme, so what you see above is exactly what prints. Use your browser's Print (or Share → Print on mobile) once you're happy with the set.
