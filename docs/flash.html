Before you click Install

  1. Use Chrome, Edge, or Firefox 151+ on a laptop or desktop — Safari doesn't support Web Serial, and no mobile browser does.
  2. Connect the board's USB-C port directly to your computer (skip hubs if the port isn't recognized).
  3. If your OS asks which driver to use, pick the one matching your board's USB-to-serial chip (usually CP210x or CH9102).
  4. Click Install, choose the matching serial port from the browser prompt, and keep the tab open until it finishes.
  5. If it asks whether to erase first, only tick that if you want a clean slate — it wipes the whole chip, including saved settings/GIFs on an SD-less S3.
