Snake Letters

Play Letters Rain

Eat the letter shown below the field and the snake grows. Touching any other letter ends the game. - shrinks the snake by 1, / halves it.

← ↑ ↓ → or W A S D steer · Space pause · Enter play · Esc menu. On a phone, tilt to steer (the starting tilt is your neutral, and pausing then resuming resets it). Turn Tilt controls off (or use a phone without a tilt sensor) to swipe on the field instead.

Without walls the snake wraps to the opposite edge.

Words list

Lowercase a-z only. Up to 24 words, 2 to 15 letters each. Words play in order and loop, with a random letter blanked each time.
