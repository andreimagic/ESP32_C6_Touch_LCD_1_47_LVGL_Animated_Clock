Tennis Letters

Play Snake Letters | Letters Rain

The ball is a letter, a to z, and it bounces off the three walls. Keep it in play with the paddle: every catch scores one and moves on to the next letter. Each bounce off the top wall speeds up both the ball and the paddle. Miss the ball and the game ends. Finish a full alphabet (26 catches) for the win tune. In Hard, catching on the left of the paddle sends the ball left and on the right sends it right, with the edges giving the steepest angle. A paddle that is moving when it catches adds spin: moving with the ball speeds it up sideways, moving against it flicks it back. The ball never goes straight up, so there is no safe spot to park. Each mode keeps its own high score.

← → or A D move the paddle (hold to keep moving) · Space pause · Enter play · ← → in the menu pick the mode · Esc menu. On a phone, tilt left and right.
