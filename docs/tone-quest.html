Tone Quest

Play Tennis Letters | Snake Letters | Letters Rain

Watch the four domes light up and listen to their tones, then repeat the sequence by rolling the ball into the same edges. Every level adds one more move to the end of the same sequence. One wrong edge ends the game. There is no winning, only how far you get: your score is the level you cleared.

Tilt (phone): tilt to roll the ball, hold it in the ring until the round starts, and after each move bring the ball back to the middle before the next edge counts. Keyboard: ← ↑ ↓ → or W A S D answer directly, Space starts the round. Touch or mouse: tap to start, swipe to answer. Esc pauses, M goes to the menu.

Controls

On iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button in Safari, then choose Add to Home Screen.

Settings

Defaults are 4, 420, 220 and 55. A lower tilt percentage means a gentler tilt is enough to reach the edges.
